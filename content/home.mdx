## What I build

Most of my work sits where machine learning meets products that have to work. At Baker Hughes I trained a document-classification model that reaches 85% accuracy. The harder part was designing the review flow around it, so a person stays in control of what gets shipped. Before that, at Juniper Networks, I built a pipeline handling more than a million network packets a day and tuned a classifier to 98% accuracy on live traffic.

I've also co-authored five peer-reviewed papers across IEEE Access and IEEE conferences, mostly on encrypted-traffic classification, intrusion detection, and cyber defense. I like the distance between a promising idea in a paper and the same idea running reliably in production. Most of what I build is aimed at closing that gap.
