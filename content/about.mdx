## How I work

I spend most of my time where software products, distributed systems, and applied machine learning overlap.

I like building things that run in real environments, where requirements change and you can't just hand something over and walk away. Clear data models, easy debugging, and systems you can actually observe matter to me more than cleverness.

I work across the stack rather than staying on one side, because the interesting problems usually live at the seams between frontend and backend. The goal is software that's simple to maintain and straightforward to hand off to whoever runs it next.
